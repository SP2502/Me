How I learn

Build. Break. Understand. Improve.

I don't always understand a system before building it. Often, building is how I begin understanding it.

I make something, find where it breaks, investigate what actually happened, and then use that understanding to improve the system.

  1. 01 Curiosity Start with a question.
  2. 02 Build Turn the question into something real.
  3. 03 Break Find what the system doesn't handle.
  4. 04 Understand Investigate the underlying mechanism.
  5. 05 Improve Build again with what I learned.
About

Curiosity turned into systems.

Who I am

I'm a Class 11 student interested in understanding how software and AI systems actually work.

Why I build

I started making projects because I wanted to grow with the world as the world is growing. Because I am curious.

Technology keeps changing, especially in AI and software. Building is my way of learning alongside that change rather than only watching it happen.

What I'm interested in

I'm interested in what happens underneath software: how APIs communicate, how services interact, how systems handle failures, how AI systems coordinate multiple steps, and how a rough experiment can become a usable system.
